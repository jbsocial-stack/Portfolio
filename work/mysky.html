Case study · Sky Group · 2023–2025

Making the MySky app feel unmistakably Sky.

As Head of Product Design & Research, I led Sky's CX Centre of Excellence in setting the strategy for the MySky app. We defined what world-class customer experience means at Sky, audited the app against it, tested new ideas with customers, and turned what we learned into features with a measurable financial return.

Role
Head of Product Design & Research
Team
CX Centre of Excellence: design & research
Scope
MySky app V3 for UK&I, DE and IT, plus a 5-year app roadmap
Methods
Brand-values audit, live vs. challenger prototype testing, quant & qual
01

The frame

World-class customer experience happens where Sky's four brand values meet.

Before changing anything, we needed a shared, simple test for "good" that designers, researchers, product and brand teams could all use. We anchored it in the brand values Sky already had, so every feature could be judged by one question: is it simplifying, welcoming, creative, and does it do the right thing?

02

Auditing the app

We scored key MySky components against the four values. Some needed a full rethink, and some just needed an uplift.

Customer Guidance Rail

An ordered list of the actions a customer must, should and could take. It was functionally sound, but it didn't feel like Sky.

  • Does the right thing. It delivers the right personalised information at the right time.
  • Simplifying. The title alone gives you everything you need at a glance.
  • Not creative. It uses an expected pattern and doesn't bring the brand to life.
  • Not welcoming. There's little focus, and no sense of settling into the experience.

Masthead navigation

The masthead should give customers a seamless, relatable and comprehensive way to navigate Sky, and it should change with the customer's state. Our proposed uplift met all four values.

  • Does the right thing. It's personalised and removes links a customer doesn't need.
  • Simplifying. A distinct, intent-led primary brand navigation.
  • Creative. A product-led sub-nav that shows every product.
  • Welcoming. Softer language, and search that asks leading questions, powered by Sky Assistant.
03

Validating with customers

Objective

To validate the strategic direction of the customer experience in the MySky app.

Method

Customers and prospects were shown the live app alongside challenger prototypes, and asked to complete tasks on both. We used a mix of quantitative and qualitative methods. Tasks covered common sales and servicing journeys, as well as new features such as content stories.

What we found

  • The challenger prototype showed potential. The data Piggybank, consolidated bill and signposted important information all performed slightly better on customer sentiment.
  • There were strategic and tactical challenges to address, specifically around navigation and engagement.
  • More research was needed into pain points and unmet needs, to deliver a joyful and meaningful experience that matches what customers expect.
04

Stories for VIP Rewards

What

Promoting regular Sky VIP Rewards through a Stories component, to increase engagement and reduce churn.

Why

We wanted more app customers using VIP, and using it more deeply. Engaged VIP members are less likely to leave. Stories tell customers about new rewards in a format they already know and enjoy.
