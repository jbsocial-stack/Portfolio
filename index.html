Selected work

Aegon UK · 2025–now AI · Fintech · 0→1

Kemble: an AI-native financial planning app for couples

I was the founding leader. I took an agentic life and financial planning product from concept to a live UK direct-to-consumer launch in four months. The market was full of single-person, single-product players, and Kemble set out to be the only joint household financial command centre.

Proposition

A three-pillar proposition: Lay It Out, Join Forces, Make It Happen. It came with a phased roadmap and a feature sequence built to hold momentum against a 12-week infrastructure lead time.

Agentic platform

Built on AWS Bedrock (Claude). A conversational agent layer with defined AI personas and a governed brand voice, a single ranked insight and next-best-action pipeline, and a Partner Integration Layer for Open Banking and regulated advice.

Trust by design

Compliance was part of the architecture from day one: a regulated-advice classification gate (REDLine), DPIA, FCA Consumer Duty and UK GDPR Art. 22 analysis. Couples each have their own account and join through shared, private-by-default "Spaces".

Research

A founding-couple beta, and a 50-couple research programme across four test conditions. I also worked with Wharton neuroscience researchers on how couples make decisions together.

Sky · 2023–25

MySky Assistant & Sky Protect

I led the teams behind Sky's AI chatbot, which cut calls by 34% and saved £5m in call costs. I also led the launch of Sky Protect, Sky's insurance and smart home vertical, and sat on Sky's AI Ethics Committee.

Sky · Design systems

Global SkyUI Design System

Delivered with the MD of Global Design and Sky Creative: the foundations, building blocks, templates and patterns that helped the company move faster. I also oversaw V3 of the MySky app across UK&I, DE and IT.

Financial Times · 2022–23

FT Edit: launch in Europe & the US

I led the design and UX research strategy, including diary studies and large user testing forums. I built a team of nine designers and wrote a 5-year design strategy for FT B2C customer products.

Vice Media · 2013–17

Vice mobile app

I was the principal design lead. I built a JTBD framework, and with the Data Science team shipped "cold start" recommendations based on sentiment. I also created a React Native design system.
